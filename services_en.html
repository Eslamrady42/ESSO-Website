Our Services

We provide a comprehensive range of engineering and consulting services to meet your project needs.

Engineering Design

We provide integrated architectural and structural design using modern design tools such as AutoCAD, Revit, and SketchUp, while considering aesthetic, functional, and economic requirements.

  • Architectural design (plans, elevations, and construction drawings)
  • Structural design (concrete and steel structures)
  • Building Information Modeling (BIM)
  • Interior design and site planning

Smart Solutions

We provide smart building and facility control systems, including Home Automation and BMS, to improve comfort, safety, monitoring, and energy efficiency.

  • Lighting and HVAC control systems
  • Security and monitoring systems (CCTV and alarm systems)
  • Energy management systems
  • System integration through IoT

Management Consulting & Project Management

We support efficient project delivery through specialized management and consulting services, from feasibility studies through final handover.

  • Feasibility studies
  • Project management (PMO)
  • Execution supervision
  • Project assessment and design review

Renewable Energy & Sustainability

We provide renewable energy solutions, including solar and wind energy, and support sustainable building approaches aligned with environmental standards such as LEED and ESTIDAMA.

  • Design, supply, and installation of solar energy systems
  • Energy efficiency studies
  • LEED and sustainability support
  • Waste management and recycling solutions

Engineering Supervision & Execution

We supervise engineering project execution to help ensure quality, compliance with approved designs and specifications, accurate follow-up, and regular progress reporting.

  • Engineering and architectural execution supervision
  • Quality control and material testing
  • Contract and contractor management
  • Progress reporting

Mechanical & Electrical Design

We provide integrated mechanical, electrical, and plumbing (MEP) designs using modern engineering tools and technologies.

  • Heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems (HVAC)
  • Electrical systems (power, lighting, and panels)
  • Plumbing and drainage systems
  • Fire protection systems
