- That field is the protobuf Schema representation and caused nested type/proto validation errors.

Fix:
- Use generationConfig.responseJsonSchema for the floor-plan JSON schema.
- Preserve standard JSON Schema types (string/number/integer/boolean/object/array and nullable unions).
- Keep additionalProperties:false where used; it is valid in responseJsonSchema.
- Keep Gemini image/PDF inline media handling and the ESSO room/BOQ rules unchanged.
