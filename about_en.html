About Us

Get to know ESSO up close: our vision, mission, and values.

Our Story

ESSO was founded in 2015 by a group of experienced engineers and consultants with extensive expertise in various engineering fields. From the very beginning, we set out to deliver innovative engineering solutions that combine high quality with modern technologies. Over the years, we have executed numerous major projects in Egypt and the Arab world, earning our clients' trust through our commitment to excellence and precision.

At ESSO, we believe that engineering is not just numbers and designs; it is an art and creativity aimed at improving people's lives. Therefore, we strive to deeply understand our clients' needs to provide customized solutions that meet their aspirations.

Our Vision & Mission

Vision: To be the leading partner in providing smart engineering solutions in the region, and to contribute to building a more sustainable and innovative future.

Mission: To deliver integrated consulting and engineering solutions using the latest global technologies, focusing on quality, innovation, and client satisfaction. We strive to develop the engineering sector by adopting concepts of artificial intelligence and the Internet of Things in our projects.

Our Values

Integrity

We adhere to the highest standards of honesty and transparency in all our dealings.

Innovation

We constantly seek new ideas to deliver unconventional solutions.

Teamwork

We believe success is achieved through collaboration between our team and our clients.

Sustainability

We consider the environment and community in every project we undertake.
