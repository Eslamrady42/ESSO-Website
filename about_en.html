About Us

Get to know ESSO, our vision, mission, and values.

Our Story

ESSO is a startup focused on smart engineering solutions, building an integrated approach that connects engineering, automation, and connected technologies for residential and commercial spaces. We focus first on understanding the client’s needs, then designing practical solutions around functionality, reliability, scalability, and budget.

At ESSO, we believe that engineering is not just numbers and designs; it is an art and creativity aimed at improving people's lives. Therefore, we work to understand each client's needs in depth and provide practical, customized solutions that fit the project.

Our Vision & Mission

Vision: To become a trusted partner for smart engineering solutions and contribute to buildings that are more efficient, comfortable, and connected.

Mission: To deliver integrated consulting and engineering solutions using appropriate, reliable technologies, with a focus on quality, clarity, and client satisfaction. We adopt automation, artificial intelligence, and the Internet of Things when they add real value to a project.

Our Values

Integrity

We adhere to the highest standards of honesty and transparency in all our dealings.

Innovation

We constantly seek new ideas to deliver unconventional solutions.

Teamwork

We believe success is achieved through collaboration between our team and our clients.

Sustainability

We consider the environment and community in every project we undertake.
